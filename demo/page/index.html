What to try
  • The empty page. Hello, Ada with the composer centred under it, and the starters beneath. Type, paste a long draft or attach a file: the composer grows and stays centred.
  • Send. Pick a starter or press Enter: the composer slides to the foot of the page and the conversation takes the space.
  • New chat. The header's + snaps back to the greeting, with focus in the composer. Reload on a conversation with messages: it mounts docked, with no centred frame first.
  • The name. Edit User name on the empty page: the greeting follows as you type. Clear it for Hello there.
  • Off, and your own. Greeting: off is the layout from before the greeting. Own greeting slots host markup with a mark in place of the text; the starters stay.
  • A phone. Open the address the demo server prints on this network, focus the composer, and check the greeting and composer stay above the on-screen keyboard.
